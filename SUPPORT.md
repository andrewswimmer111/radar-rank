# RadarRank Support

Need help, hit a bug, or want to request a feature? Email
<mtaibuilds@gmail.com> and I'll get back to you within a few days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data stored?

Everything you create in RadarRank — collections, templates, evaluations,
scores — lives on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you tap
**Share** on an evaluation, and even then only that specific evaluation
is uploaded so voters can access it via the link.

See the [privacy policy](./PRIVACY) for full details.

### How does the share feature work?

Tap Share on an evaluation and RadarRank generates a link. Anyone with
that link can open a voting page in their browser, submit a name, and
score each participant on your rubric. Their votes appear inside your
app when they're done. Only people you send the link to can access the
evaluation — links are unguessable and there is no public directory.

### How do I stop sharing an evaluation?

Open the shared evaluation and tap **Unshare**. The evaluation is
immediately removed from the server and the link stops working.
Deleting the evaluation entirely also removes any associated shared
data.

### Can I export my evaluations?

You can save any evaluation as a shareable card image to your Photos
library from the evaluation screen. Full data export is not yet
available — email me if this is something you need.

### The app crashes on launch or a feature isn't working

Please email <mtaibuilds@gmail.com> with:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
- What you were trying to do when it happened
- Any error messages you saw

Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.

### How do I request a feature?

Email <mtaibuilds@gmail.com> with what you're trying to do and why the
current app doesn't handle it well. Concrete scenarios are more useful
than abstract feature names.

## Reporting a security or privacy issue

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability or a privacy issue
in RadarRank, email <mtaibuilds@gmail.com> with details. Please don't
disclose the issue publicly until it's resolved.
